Across the county

Three kinds of Cheshire job, one way of working.

Cheshire work comes in three shapes. One-off new homes on plots in and around Knutsford, Mobberley and the villages, where the Green Belt, replacement-dwelling rules and conservation areas shape what can be built. Whole-house renovations and large extensions of the Edwardian and 1930s houses in Hale, Bowdon, Wilmslow and Alderley Edge. And basements beneath existing houses where the plot cannot grow outwards.

Our Knutsford new build, Wilmslow house and Hale basement are each on the work pages with photographs and values. Planning across this area is split between Trafford, Cheshire East and Warrington councils, so we start every job by checking which rules apply to the address.

Straight answers

Questions we get asked.

Everywhere within about half an hour of Altrincham: Hale, Bowdon, Hale Barns, Timperley, Lymm, Wilmslow, Alderley Edge, Handforth, Knutsford, Mobberley, Mere, Prestbury, Macclesfield and Northwich. Further afield by arrangement.

Often, within limits. Replacement dwellings, extensions within the size tests and infill inside village boundaries are regularly approved; new homes on open Green Belt land are not. We will give you a straight answer on a plot before you commit to it. Our guides to buying a plot in Cheshire and to replacement dwellings explain the tests.

Our residential work is substantial projects: extensions and remodels from around £100,000, whole-house renovations, basements and new homes. For a single steel beam or a knock-through, our sister company The Load Bearing Company (www.theloadbearingcompany.co.uk) is the right call.

Tatton Projects is a Federation of Master Builders member, Constructionline Gold and CITB registered, with CSCS-carded site staff.
