Questions

Common questions

What types of suspended ceiling do you install?

Exposed-grid tile ceilings, concealed and MF plasterboard ceilings, bulkheads, acoustic rafts and fire-rated ceilings.

Do you coordinate with M&E contractors?

Yes. We agree setting-out and service positions before install so lights, diffusers and sprinkler heads land in the grid.

Can you replace ceilings in a live office?

Yes. We phase ceiling replacements around occupied floors and work out of hours where the client needs it.
