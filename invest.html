How a deal works

No pool. No hype.
One project, in writing.

The same discipline that keeps our final accounts where they were promised. Because there's no history to point at, we lead on structure instead — how your money is protected and how you get paid.

01

Appraisal first

Before you commit a penny, you get the full appraisal on that specific scheme: land or purchase cost, build cost, end value, timeline and the abnormals. If the numbers don't work, we'll tell you — that advice is free.

02

Terms in writing

Profit share, interest, or equity split — whatever the deal is, it's agreed and signed before work starts. No verbal understandings. What a return looks like is specific to each project and set out in that deal's terms, not promised in advance on a website.

03

Security & control

Where the structure allows, security is taken over the asset. You get monthly valuations and clear drawdown — you always know where the number is, the same way our clients do on a build.

04

Build & exit

Owner-managed delivery through to completion. Capital and any agreed return are settled on the project's exit — sale or refinance. Your money is committed until then; these deals are not liquid.

Before you go further

The risks.
Stated plainly.

The same straight answers we give on a build. If any of these would sink you, this isn't for you — and we'd rather you knew now.

You could lose everything

Property development can and does lose money. If a scheme fails, values fall, or costs overrun, you may get back less than you put in — or nothing. Only invest money you can afford to lose entirely.

No track record yet

This is a new activity for us. We have a strong building record, but no history of delivering returns to investors. Don't treat our construction figures as any indication of investment performance — they aren't one.

Your money is locked in

These aren't savings accounts. Capital is committed until the project completes and sells or refinances. There's no early exit, and timelines can slip — planning, weather, market and build all move the date you get paid.

No FSCS safety net

Unlike a bank deposit, this is not protected by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme. If things go wrong, you're unlikely to have recourse to a compensation scheme.

Nothing here is advice

This page is not financial, tax or legal advice, and it's not a personal recommendation. Take independent professional advice before committing to anything.

Tax is on you

The tax treatment of any return depends on your circumstances and can change. We can't advise on it — speak to your accountant before you invest.
