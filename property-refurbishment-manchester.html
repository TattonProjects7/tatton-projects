Manchester refurbishment

Old buildings, made to work.

Manchester's housing is mostly old: Victorian terraces and villas, Edwardian semis and 1930s houses, many split into flats and put back together, with services and structure that have been patched for decades. A proper refurbishment fixes the bones first, then the rooms.

In Didsbury we've refurbished through houses alongside two kitchen extensions: herringbone floors and panelling, large-format tile, fitted wardrobes, new staircase railings and joinery throughout. On the commercial side we refurbished a £175,000 service centre in Stockport without it closing for a day, and a £125,000 office in Stockport finished to the boardroom.
