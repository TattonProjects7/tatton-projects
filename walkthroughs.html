Walkthroughs · FAQ

What people ask about these videos.

Where can I watch Tatton Projects site walkthrough videos?

Our full walkthrough library lives on this page. We publish site walkthrough videos of our live and completed projects — basement conversions in Hale, new-build family homes in Knutsford, whole-house renovations in Wilmslow, Cat B office fit-outs in Liverpool and housing developments across Cheshire.

What kind of projects do Tatton Projects film?

We film one-off new build homes (£400k–£3m+), housing developments of two to thirty plots, basement and building conversions, and commercial Cat A and Cat B fit-out across the UK. The walkthroughs we publish are the way we hand projects back to clients at the end of the programme.

Can I share these walkthrough videos on social media?

Yes — each video on this page has Open Graph and Twitter player tags so when the page URL is pasted into LinkedIn, X, Facebook or WhatsApp, a video card is generated automatically. You can also link directly to a single video using the anchor fragments (#basement-hale, #knutsford-new-build, #wilmslow-before, #wilmslow-mid, #wilmslow-finished, #office-fit-out-liverpool, #multi-site, #housing-development).

Do you cover basement conversions in Cheshire?

Yes. Our Hale basement walkthrough shows a full below-ground project built beneath an existing property — structural underpinning, tanking and waterproofing, services and finished living space. We take basement work across Altrincham, Hale, Bowdon, Knutsford, Wilmslow and surrounding Cheshire locations.

Can I see a new build go from plot to completion?

Yes. The Knutsford walkthrough runs the whole arc of the build — empty birch-lined plot, structure going up in knapped flint and red brick under scaffolding, then the finished two-storey detached in daylight with slate roof, oak porch and gravel drive.

Do you deliver office fit-outs outside Manchester?

Yes. Our Sentric Music walkthrough is a Cat B fit-out in Liverpool. We deliver Cat A and Cat B fit-out UK-wide on selected projects, with pre-construction and site teams run out of Altrincham.

How often do you add new walkthroughs?

We publish fresh walkthroughs at project handover — roughly once a month during the busy season. Follow us on LinkedIn or drop us a note to be told when the next one goes up.
