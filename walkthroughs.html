As a 2026 working guide, a new basement beneath an existing house runs around £3,000–£5,000 per square metre of finished floor area, excluding VAT, professional fees and abnormals. Ground conditions, the water table and how much underpinning the existing structure needs move the number most, which is why anyone quoting a single figure before seeing the site is guessing. Send us the address and the drawings and you'll get a written elemental cost plan instead of a range. See the full cost guide →
