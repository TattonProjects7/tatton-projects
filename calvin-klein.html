Delivered by Dave Groom as project and contracts manager, prior to founding Tatton Projects in 2020.

Shell to shop. This one has the whole story in the photographs — bare concrete and blockwork, services first fix, ceilings, feature lighting, then a finished store trading behind a branded hoarding that never came down until it did.

Delivered inside a live shopping centre. Every delivery out of hours, through service corridors, around a mall full of shoppers.
