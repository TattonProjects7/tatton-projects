Straight answers

Questions we get asked.

It depends on the size of the house and how much structure changes, but a full renovation of a four-bedroom house, back to brick with new services, typically runs several months on site. We give you a dated programme with the cost plan and update it as the job progresses.

For a full back-to-brick renovation it is usually better and cheaper to move out: the house has no services for a period and every room is a work area. For phased work we can plan around an occupied house, which we do routinely on commercial jobs.

Internal renovation rarely needs planning permission, but extensions, loft and basement conversions and changes to a listed building or a house in a conservation area can. All structural and most services work needs Building Control approval, which we arrange and manage as part of the contract.
