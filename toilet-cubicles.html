Toilet Cubicles & Washrooms packages

  • Full-height and standard toilet cubicles in compact laminate (HPL)
  • IPS framing and duct panels to conceal cisterns and pipework, with access panels
  • Vanity units with solid-surface tops and undermount basin cut-outs
  • Washroom wall panelling and IPS back panels
  • Mirrors, dispensers and washroom accessories fitted
  • Shower and changing room cubicles and benching
  • Coordinated with plumbing and M&E first and second fix
  • Washroom refurbishment in occupied buildings, phased floor by floor

Questions

Common questions

Do you install toilet cubicles labour only?

Yes. We install client-supplied cubicles, IPS and vanities labour only, or supply and fit the full washroom package.

Do you build the IPS framing?

Yes. We build the IPS framing at first fix, coordinate with the plumber for cisterns and pipework, then fit the duct panels, access panels and vanity tops at second fix.

Can you refurbish washrooms in a building that stays open?

Yes. We phase washroom refurbishments floor by floor so the building always has working toilets, with temporary screening and out-of-hours noisy work where needed.
