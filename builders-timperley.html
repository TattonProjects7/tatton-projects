Local contractor

The 1930s house is our home ground.

Most of Timperley was built between the wars: three-bed semis and four-bed detached houses with bay windows, a side passage and a garage no modern car fits in. They respond well to the work we do most: a full-width rear extension, the garage and side passage brought into the plan, steels in so the ground floor opens up, and a first-floor extension over the top for another bedroom and bathroom. Our Wilmslow house is exactly that kind of job, from a tired 1930s detached to a finished family home.

We also take on whole-house renovations, basements beneath existing houses and one-off new homes on infill plots, and we fit out offices for businesses in Timperley and Altrincham. Projects start at around £100,000. The person who prices the job runs it on site, and the office on The Downs is five minutes away.

Straight answers

Questions we get asked.

Suite 15, Peel House, 30 The Downs, Altrincham WA14 2PX, about a mile and a half from Timperley village. Call 0161 706 2907 or email info@tattonprojects.co.uk.

Many single-storey rear extensions fall within permitted development. Two-storey extensions, side extensions, corner plots and houses where permitted development rights have been removed usually need an application to Trafford Council. We check the address and advise before you spend money on drawings; our guide to planning permission for extensions covers the detail.

Our residential work is substantial projects: extensions and remodels from around £100,000, whole-house renovations, basements and new homes. For a single steel beam or a knock-through, our sister company The Load Bearing Company (www.theloadbearingcompany.co.uk) is the right call.

A single-storey rear extension is typically twelve to sixteen weeks on site. A two-storey extension with internal remodelling is usually five to seven months. We agree the programme with you before we start.

Send the drawings if you have them, or just the address and what you want from the house. You'll get a written cost plan from the person who would run the job, and we can meet at the property. We reply within one working day.
