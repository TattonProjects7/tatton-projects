On site

Acoustic walls

Built to the sound rating, not just the drawing.

Meeting rooms, boardrooms, treatment rooms and hotel bedrooms only work if the wall stops the sound. We build acoustic partitions to the Rw rating in your specification, using the tested manufacturer system that achieves it, and we detail the junctions where most walls leak.

  • Built to the specified Rw (dB) rating using tested British Gypsum, Knauf and Siniat systems
  • Twin-frame, staggered and wide-stud builds with acoustic insulation in the cavity
  • Double-layer acoustic plasterboard with staggered joints
  • Acoustic sealant at heads, bases and abutments; head deflection details that keep the seal
  • Flanking control: barriers in ceiling voids and raised floors, sealed service penetrations
  • Acoustic doorsets and acoustic glazing coordinated with the wall
  • Acoustic felt and timber slat wall panelling for room acoustics
  • Pre-completion sound testing arranged where the spec or Approved Document E needs it

Questions

Common questions

Can you build acoustic walls to a set sound rating?

Yes. We build acoustic partitions to the Rw rating in your specification, using the manufacturer system that achieves it: twin or staggered studs, acoustic insulation, double-layer acoustic board, sealed heads and junctions, and flanking details above the ceiling. We can arrange pre-completion sound testing.

Do you install fire-rated partitions?

Yes. We build fire-rated partitions to the specified manufacturer system, including head deflection details and fire stopping at junctions and penetrations.

Can you set out from drawings?

Yes. Our supervisors set out partition lines from your general arrangement and setting-out drawings and raise any clashes before boarding.

Do you do partitions as a separate package?

Yes: partitions on their own, or combined with drylining, ceilings, joinery and glazing as one interiors package.
