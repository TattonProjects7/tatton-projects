Questions

Common questions

Do you install fire-rated partitions?

Yes. We build fire-rated partitions to the specified manufacturer system, including head deflection details and fire stopping at junctions and penetrations.

Can you set out from drawings?

Yes. Our supervisors set out partition lines from your general arrangement and setting-out drawings and raise any clashes before boarding.

Do you do partitions as a separate package?

Yes: partitions on their own, or combined with drylining, ceilings, joinery and glazing as one interiors package.
