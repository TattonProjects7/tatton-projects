One contractor, both kinds of work

Commercial fit-out in the city, houses in the suburbs.

In Manchester we are two things at once. For landlords, occupiers and operators we are a fit-out and refurbishment contractor: Cat A and Cat B office fit-out, shopfitting, EPC and MEES upgrades and refurbishment of buildings that cannot close, from the city centre out to Stockport, Salford and Trafford Park. For homeowners we are the builders who extended and remodelled two houses in Didsbury, and who take on substantial extensions and renovations across south Manchester.

Both sides are run the same way: the person who priced the job manages it on site, the programme is agreed before we start, and the cost plan is written down. Our office on The Downs in Altrincham is twenty minutes from Deansgate and ten from Didsbury.
