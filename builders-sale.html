Local contractor

Ten minutes from Sale, every working day.

Sale's housing suits the work we do best: Victorian and Edwardian semis and terraces around Brooklands and Sale Moor, 1930s semis out towards Ashton upon Mersey and Timperley, and larger detached houses on the roads off Marsland Road and Washway Road. Most were built with a narrow back kitchen and a side return, which is why rear and wrap-around extensions are the job we are asked about most.

We take on substantial projects: extensions and remodels from around £100,000, whole-house renovations, cellar and basement conversions, one-off new homes, and Cat A and Cat B office fit-out for businesses in Sale town centre and along the A56. The person who prices your job runs it on site, and the office is close enough to call in.

What we do

What we build in Sale.

01

Rear and wrap-around extensions

Single and two-storey extensions on Victorian, Edwardian and 1930s houses: side returns filled in, kitchens opened to the garden, steels in and walls out, with the structure designed and signed off properly.

02

Whole-house renovation

Older Sale houses taken back to brick and rebuilt as one programme: rewire, replumb, insulation, glazing, kitchens, bathrooms, staircases and joinery.

03

Cellars and basements

Victorian cellars underpinned, tanked and finished as usable rooms, as on our Hale basement conversion.

04

Office and shop fit-out

Cat A and Cat B office fit-out and shopfitting for businesses in Sale town centre, Sale Moor and along the A56, often while the business keeps trading.
