Retail shopfit

Fossil

Location London Client Fossil

Delivered by Dave Groom as project and contracts manager, prior to founding Tatton Projects in 2020.

A brand-standard retail fit-out in London. Every element built to a global specification and signed off against it — illuminated watch walls, glass display cases, a steel-and-timber shelving system, lacquered joinery, and a media wall behind the cash desk.

Retail is unforgiving. The brand team measure the finishes, the trading date does not move, and the shopping centre only lets you in at night.
