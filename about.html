About Tatton Interiors

The interiors arm of Tatton Projects, set up so main contractors get a dedicated interiors subcontractor with a main contractor's backing.

Why a separate interiors company

Tatton Projects builds new homes, developments and commercial fit-outs as a main contractor. More and more main contractors asked us to take on their drylining, ceilings, partitions, joinery and glazing packages. Tatton Interiors exists to do exactly that, with its own focus, its own teams and the same standards.

Who runs it

Tatton Interiors is run by founder Dave Groom, who has 20+ years in construction. He started on the tools as a joiner and worked up through site manager, project manager and contracts manager roles, including McGoff Construction and Morris & Spottiswood, before founding Tatton Projects. You deal with the person who prices your package.

Tatton Interiors is a UK interior fit-out subcontractor working for main contractors, fit-out firms and developers across England, Scotland and Wales.
