Manchester extensions

The back of the house, opened up.

A Manchester extension is usually about the kitchen: knocking the back rooms through, filling the side return of a terrace, or running a full-width extension across a semi so the kitchen, dining and living space look onto the garden. That means steels, new openings, drainage, a roof that lets light in, and a kitchen that fits the space it's built for.

In Didsbury we built a £160,000 rear extension with a bookmatched marble island and minimal-frame sliders, and replaced the staircase railings through the house. Nearby, a £120,000 kitchen extension sits under a full-length glazed roof with a crittall-style screen to the garden, herringbone floors and large-format tile run through. Both were priced and run on site by the same person.
