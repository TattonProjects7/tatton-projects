Wilmslow

A different house on the same footprint.

Our Wilmslow job is the one to look at if you own a tired 1930s house there. It was dark and cut up inside. We extended it front and rear, re-rendered it, replaced every window, added a full-width rear extension with bi-folds onto a new terrace, opened the ground floor into one kitchen-living space, and built a new oak staircase and oak-framed porch. It was delivered and settled at the agreed final account.

We're based at Peel House on The Downs in Altrincham and take on substantial projects: extensions and remodels from around £100,000, whole-house renovations, basements and one-off new homes across Wilmslow, Alderley Edge and Handforth. The person who prices your job runs it on site.
