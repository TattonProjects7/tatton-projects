Urmston

More room at the back.

Urmston's semis and terraces usually want the same thing: a bigger kitchen opened onto the garden, and a house brought up to date. Our Didsbury extensions, at circa £120,000 and £160,000, show the standard: a kitchen opened to the garden, rooflights and minimal-frame glazing.

Most Urmston extensions take one of three planning routes: up to 3 m at the back under permitted development, up to 6 m through Trafford's prior approval, or a full application for most two-storey work and anything in the Flixton conservation area. Prior approval gives the neighbours at least 21 days and can take up to 42, so we build that wait into the programme before booking trades.

Opening the back of a 1930s semi almost always means a steel where the rear wall was, often a second where an internal wall comes out, and a party wall notice to next door. We take on substantial projects from around £100,000, and the person who prices your job runs it on site.
