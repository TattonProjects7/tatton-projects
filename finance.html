How it works

Four steps.
No bank queue.

We price the project. Our finance partner finds the money. You deal with decision-makers at both ends — no call centres on either side.

01

We price the project

A fixed-price quote with the working shown, exactly as we always do. You accept our building contract, and that fixed price is what the finance is arranged against.

02

Apply in minutes

With your contract in place, we introduce you to Ideal4Finance, our FCA-regulated finance partner. Their secure online application takes minutes, and their finance experts handle the paperwork from there.

03

A panel of lenders competes

Ideal4Finance searches a panel of lenders representing a fair analysis of the market to find finance that suits your circumstances. Decisions are quick — often hours, not weeks.

04

We build. The lender handles the rest.

Finance approved, programme agreed, site started. More than half of the finance is payable to us upfront when the funds come through, and the balance follows your contract's payment schedule. Repayments run between you and the lender — one point of contact for the build, a clear agreement for the money.

The facts

Five things to know

  1. FCA-regulated partner. Ideal4Finance (Ideal Sales Solutions Ltd) is a licensed credit broker — not a lender — authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, FRN 703401.
  2. A panel, not one bank. They source loans from a panel of lenders representing a fair analysis of the market, matched to what you disclose to them.
  3. Decisions in hours. Their lenders make quick decisions, so your project can move in days rather than waiting weeks for a bank.
  4. Commercial funds paid on delivery. On commercial finance, funds are paid directly on delivery of the work — the lender then looks after your repayments.
  5. Experts handle the paperwork. Once introduced, you're in the hands of their finance team. We get on with the thing we're good at: the build.

Full transparency

Where we stand

  1. We're the builder. Tatton Projects acts as an introducer to Ideal4Finance. We are not a lender or a credit broker, and we don't give financial advice.
  2. Commission, declared. Ideal4Finance receives a commission from its lenders when a loan completes, and Tatton Projects may receive a commission as the introducer. Neither ever changes the price of your project.
  3. The quote is the quote. Finance or no finance, our fixed price stands. We'd rather lose a finance commission than inflate a build price — that defeats the whole point.
  4. Secured loans carry a warning. Think carefully before securing debts against your home. Your home may be repossessed if you do not keep up repayments on a mortgage or any other debt secured on it.
