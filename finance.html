Your options

The right finance for the size of the job.

The amount you borrow decides the type of loan. We'll point you to the right one when we quote.

£1,000 – £25,000

Personal loan
(unsecured)

For smaller works, SiloPod garden rooms and acoustic pods, kitchens and refurbishments. Not secured on your home.

  • Terms typically 1 to 10 years
  • Soft-search eligibility check
  • Decision in minutes
  • No need to own your home

£25,000 – £100,000

Homeowner loan
(secured)

For extensions, basement conversions and whole-house renovations. Secured on your home, which usually means lower rates and longer terms.

  • Terms typically up to 25 years
  • Keeps your existing mortgage in place
  • For homeowners with equity in their property
  • Your home may be repossessed if you do not keep up repayments

Businesses

Fit-out
finance

For Cat A and Cat B office fit-out, retail, hospitality and healthcare. Your business spreads the cost monthly over a fixed term instead of paying up front.

  • Lease or hire purchase
  • Typically 2 to 5 years
  • Payments may be tax-deductible
  • Keeps working capital free
