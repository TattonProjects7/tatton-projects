Questions

Common questions

What does Tatton Interiors do?

Tatton Interiors is an interior fit-out subcontractor. We price and deliver plastering and drylining, metal stud partitions, suspended ceilings, joinery and fitted furniture, and internal glazing packages for main contractors and fit-out firms.

Do you work supply & fit or labour only?

Both. Every package can be priced supply & fit with materials included, or labour only where the main contractor free-issues materials.

Where do you work?

We are based in Altrincham, Greater Manchester, and work across Manchester, Cheshire, Merseyside, Lancashire, Yorkshire and the wider North West, and take packages UK-wide.

How is Tatton Interiors related to Tatton Projects?

Tatton Interiors is the interiors subcontracting arm of Tatton Projects. It is a trading name of Tatton Projects of Cheshire Limited and part of the DPG Holdings group. Tatton Projects delivers main contracts; Tatton Interiors delivers interior packages for other contractors.

Are you pre-qualified?

Yes. We hold Constructionline Gold and SSIP accreditation, are CITB registered, and our site teams are CSCS carded. We will complete your PQQ or supplier forms.
