Glazing systems

Which glass partition fits the spec?

Illustrations of the systems we install. Typical performance shown; the final figure always comes from the specified manufacturer system.

Frameless glass

Butt-jointed toughened or laminated glass in slim head and floor channels. Clean sightlines for meeting rooms and receptions.

  • Single glazed, 10–12mm
  • Typically around Rw 35–38 dB
  • Frameless or framed glass doors

Framed aluminium

Aluminium-framed single or double-glazed screens with integrated doors and blinds options. The workhorse for offices and healthcare.

  • Single or double glazed
  • Double glazed typically Rw 42–50 dB+
  • Fire-rated options available

Crittall-style screens

Slim black steel-look frames with glazing bars, for a warehouse feel in offices, hospitality and high-end residential.

  • Steel or aluminium steel-look frames
  • Single, double and acoustic glass
  • Hinged, pivot and sliding doors

Questions

Common questions

Do you install frameless glass partitions?

Yes. We install frameless single and double glazed partitions, glazed doors and meeting rooms, including acoustic-rated glazing.

Can you do glazing with the partitions package?

Yes. Running glazing alongside partitions and ceilings means one team owns the junctions between them.

Do you supply manifestation?

Yes. We supply and fit manifestation and privacy film to meet Building Regulations and the client brand.
